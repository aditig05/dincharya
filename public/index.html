Your day, by voice.

Say what you're doing, in Hindi or English. Dincharya records when, and keeps your day on every device.

  • “Break for 30 min” → a timed break with a countdown
  • “2 se 5 tak DSA kiya” → logged 2–5 pm on your timeline
  • Weekly view, streaks, and a spoken summary of your day
  • Ask “is hafte kitna padha?” and hear the answer

Your log is private to your Google account. Dincharya only sees your name, email and photo.

Put Dincharya on your home screen for one-tap logging and alerts.
