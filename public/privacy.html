Privacy policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

Dincharya is a personal daily log. You tell it what you are doing, and it records when. This page explains what data that involves and what happens to it.

What we collect

  • Your Google account basics: your name, email address, profile photo and Google account ID, received when you choose “Continue with Google”. We never see your Google password.
  • Your log: the activities you record: what, when, how long, its type and any note.
  • Your settings: goals, quick buttons and language.
  • Voice recordings and sentences you type when you use the mic or the text box. These are sent for processing (see below) and are not stored by Dincharya.

How it is used

Only to run the app for you: showing your timeline, weekly view, goals and summaries, answering your questions, and keeping your log the same on all your devices. We do not sell your data, show ads, or use your log to train AI models.

Who processes it

  • AI services turn speech into text, understand your sentences and read replies aloud: Sarvam AI first, and if it is unavailable, Groq and Google Gemini. They receive the recording or text needed for that one request, under their own privacy terms.
  • Vercel hosts the app, and a Postgres database (Neon) stores your account and log.
  • Google provides sign-in.

Storage and security

Your log is stored in the database under your account, and a copy is kept in your browser so the app works offline. Connections are encrypted (HTTPS). Your session is a signed cookie that scripts on the page cannot read.

Your choices

  • Download all your data from Settings at any time (JSON or CSV).
  • Sign out to remove the log from that device. It stays in your account.
  • Delete account in Settings permanently removes your account, log and settings from our database.

Children

Dincharya is not intended for children under 13.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above will change. Questions or requests: open an issue on GitHub.
